Google Spell Check 2.2.1
========================

"Google Spell Check: Fix" is back to touching nothing but the spelling.

2.2.0 briefly had it move your selection onto the words it corrected. That's useful
feedback but it's a surprise from a command whose job is to fix spelling quietly, and it
made the next keystroke overwrite the correction.

If you do want to see what changed, that's what "Google Spell Check: Fix and Highlight"
is for -- same edits, each correction squiggled and annotated.
