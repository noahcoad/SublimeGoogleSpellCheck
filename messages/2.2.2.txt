Google Spell Check 2.2.2
========================

Fixes misspellings that Google had quietly stopped correcting.

Google's suggestions changed, and some common typos only show up flagged inside a
longer search completion. "recieve" was left as-is (#32), and so was "alot of people".
Both are corrected again: "receive", "a lot of people".

Only the part you typed is changed. Anything Google corrects further along in its own
completion is ignored.
